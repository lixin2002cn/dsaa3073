    Weekly Validation Sheets

    A Validation Sheet is provided every week to help students check their understanding. Validation Sheets and Open Investigations are distinct from the timed Part Tests and do not contribute to the course grade unless announced otherwise.

    Part Tests

    Item Arrangement
    Number of Tests 5, one after each Part
    Timing The next class after the Part is completed
    Duration 30 minutes per Test
    Marks 120 marks per Test
    Format 4 multiple-choice questions and 6 short-answer questions
    Total raw marks 600 marks
    Course weighting 14% per Test; 70% in total

    Student Presentation

    Student presentations take place in Weeks 11–13 and contribute 30% of the course grade. Each student presents an individually completed written assignment or programming project that has been approved by the instructor in advance. See the Assignment page for possible directions.

    The detailed rubric, submission method, and student order are TBD. The assignment/project is expected to be submitted by the middle of the examination period.

    Grade Descriptors

    Grade Performance Level Description
    A Excellent Demonstrates comprehensive understanding of learning theory and can construct rigorous proofs, apply guarantees fluently, and synthesize concepts across Parts.
    B Good Shows solid understanding of core concepts and correctly applies techniques to most problems, with only minor gaps.
    C Satisfactory Demonstrates adequate knowledge of fundamental definitions and theorems and can solve routine problems.
    D Marginal Pass Shows threshold understanding of basic concepts but has difficulty with proofs and theoretical analysis.
    F Fail Demonstrates insufficient understanding of core concepts and does not meet minimum requirements.

    Learning Format

    This course uses a self-paced, assisted learning approach:

    1. Self-directed study with comprehensive Learning Sheets.
    2. Weekly Validation Sheets for checking understanding.
    3. Weekly Open Investigations for research-oriented exploration.
    4. Five 30-minute Part Tests, held after the corresponding Parts.
    5. Instructor and TA support during scheduled class and Tutorial time.
    6. Student presentations in Weeks 11–13.
